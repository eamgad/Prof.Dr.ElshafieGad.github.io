About

I am a Doctor of Physical Chemistry and Professor of Petroleum & Petrochemicals at the Egyptian Petroleum Research Institute (EPRI), Nasr City, Cairo. My field is applied physical chemistry: petroleum refining, petrochemicals, polymers and surface-active agents.

I have led research and industrial development on polymer-cement composites, concrete superplasticizers, and nanoparticle and surfactant interactions. My current interests are computational science and statistical analysis.

Research

  • Surfactants and colloids

    Surface and thermodynamic properties of cationic, anionic and nonionic surfactants, micellization, HLB and CMC, and biodegradable surfactants from natural products.

  • Cement, concrete and polymers

    Superplasticizers and admixtures, polymer-impregnated cement pastes, rheology, and thermal decomposition of cement pastes. Stabilization and electrical properties of poly(vinyl chloride).

  • Corrosion inhibition and adsorption

    Surfactants and drug molecules as corrosion inhibitors for steel and aluminium, studied by experiment and DFT. Adsorption on carbon nanotubes and graphene.

  • Petroleum and petrochemicals

    Flow improvers and pour point depressants for waxy crudes, emulsion stability, and direct conversion of natural gas to petrochemicals.

  • Computational chemistry

    QSPR models for HLB, flash point and octane number, molecular docking, and kinetic analysis of solid-state decomposition.
